# PORTABOOM Incredible QR

Living3 Incredible QR for PORTABOOM PB4000 (ICQR Magic Tree *pattern*: a 3D brand world that is itself scannable).

- **Default** = living 3D PORTABOOM world (powder orange plaza, twin-tidy5 PB4000).
- **`?showtime=1`** (and `?v=living3c&showtime=1`) auto-plays ~7s — longer than the living2 teaser GIF loop (~3.6s). Clear amber, then red as the boom lowers. After the boom is down, the page goes to DEST.
- The scannable matrix lives in the terrain. Tap / **Tap to scan** for a clearer scan pose (DEST product page).
- Print PNG is a demoted export under the scan dock — never the READY claim
- DEST: https://www.trafficaccess.com.au/portaboom-product/portaboom-pb4000-series/

Cache-bust: `app.js?v=living3c`

Pages: https://tas-fdnxt.github.io/portaboom-incredible-qr/

Send QR (after Chief merge): `fabian-showtime-qr.png` → `https://tas-fdnxt.github.io/portaboom-incredible-qr/?v=living3c&showtime=1`

## How to scan

1. Phone camera on the stationary send QR opens this living page with showtime.
2. After the boom drop, the page automatically opens DEST.
3. On the default living page (no `showtime=`), tap the scene or **Tap to scan**, then point a phone at the field (DEST).
4. Drag to orbit in the world. Print PNG is a paper export only.
