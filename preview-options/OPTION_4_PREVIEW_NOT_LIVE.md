## URL compression hole

`https://tas-fdnxt.github.io/portaboom-incredible-qr/?v=living10&showtime=1`

is almost certainly over that budget (host + path + query). A live clip would need a short registered invocation host, then redirect into the living tip. That is a later product decision, not a PREVIEW claim.
